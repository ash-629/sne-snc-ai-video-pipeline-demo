# Image generation prompts

Both project images were generated with Codex built-in image generation. The final cover was generated as one unified image with characters, scene, sticker outlines, shadows and all formal text together. No formal cover text was added afterward; the generated cover was only resized to 1280×720.

## Cover

- Use case: `ads-marketing`
- References: `小悦_三视图.png` and `Leo_三视图.png` for identity only; the final covers from `0827 你先忙`, `0828 来都来了`, and `0831 没事` for layout, brightness, hierarchy and sticker treatment only.
- Scene: Xiao Yue on the left holding two cake slices that are almost but not exactly the same size; Leo on the right, confused and comparing their size with his hands; warm dessert shop.
- Style: bright polished semi-realistic anime YouTube education thumbnail, thick white character outlines and soft shadows, readable at 320 px wide.
- Text, generated verbatim in the image:
  - `ALMOST? SAME? GOOD ENOUGH?`
  - `chà bu duō`
  - `差不多`
  - `一个词，四种用法`
  - `yí ge cí, sì zhǒng yòng fǎ`
  - `ONE PHRASE, FOUR USES`
  - `HSK 4-5`
  - `@SIMPLY NATURAL CHINESE`
- Constraints: no extra text, no gibberish, no duplicated wording, no cropped copy, no childlike/chibi proportions, and no identity blending.

## Background base

- Use case: `illustration-story`
- Reference: this episode's completed cover for atmosphere and palette only.
- Scene: empty warm dessert shop at evening, cake display in the distance, broad marble table, two subtly different strawberry cake slices, two teacups and a kitchen timer.
- Composition: 16:9, broad uncluttered lower half for the subtitle panel and a simple upper-right area for the video window.
- Constraints: no people, no text, no signs, no logos and no watermark.
